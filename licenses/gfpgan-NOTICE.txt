FFHQ five-point alignment template follows facexlib's FaceRestoreHelper:
https://github.com/xinntao/facexlib/blob/master/facexlib/utils/face_restoration_helper.py

GFPGAN v1.4 estimates face detail. Des Studio limits the blend, checks a
temporary local SFace descriptor, and requires preview review before Apply.
Neither restoration nor the face-match check guarantees identity or recovered
ground truth. No image or face descriptor is uploaded.
